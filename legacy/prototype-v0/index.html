                How the analysis works

                Each reading is checked two ways: against general adult ranges, and against the astronaut's own baseline (mean and spread of their previous readings, once at least 5 exist). A reading 2 or more standard deviations from baseline is flagged "Watch"; it becomes "Alert" when it is 3 or more standard deviations off and also outside the general range, or far outside the general range on its own. Personal baselines matter in space: body temperature, blood pressure and heart rate shift in microgravity, so Earth ranges alone can miss or over-flag changes. Trend checks look for patterns such as several nights under 6 hours of sleep, which NASA's Reaction Self Test study linked to slower responses and higher stress.

                Not a medical device. Ranges are general adult guidance. Always follow flight rules and the Crew Medical Officer / flight surgeon.

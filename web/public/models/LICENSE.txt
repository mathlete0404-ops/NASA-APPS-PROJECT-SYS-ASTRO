3D anatomy models in this folder (skeleton.glb, muscles.glb, skin.glb, nerves.glb, joints.glb)

Derived from:
  "Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0"
  https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
which is derived from:
  "BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan"
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html

Changes made by Team SYS-ASTRO (Crew Mirror): selected structures exported with
ingest/blender/export_body.py — helper objects, labels and sex-specific anatomy removed,
meshes decimated and Draco-compressed, body-region tags added.
